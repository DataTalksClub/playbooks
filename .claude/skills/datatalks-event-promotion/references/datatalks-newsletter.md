# DataTalks.Club Newsletter

Use this reference for copy sent by DataTalks.Club through its newsletter system. Write in the organizational, community-oriented voice from `datatalks-style.md`, without a personal narrator.

## Weekly Issue Slots

Event mentions, event detail blocks, sponsored promo blocks, and Alexey newsletter post mentions are slots in the weekly issue. Their formats live in the `dtc-newsletter` skill (`references/slots.md`). When a campaign needs newsletter copy, draft it in that slot format so it drops into the next issue unchanged:

- Ordinary event: a line in the Upcoming events list.
- Workshop, launch stream, or featured event: an event detail slot.
- Sponsored event or workshop: a promo slot marked as sponsored content.

## Registrant Follow-Up

Use this after the event for people who registered:

- Acknowledge the event directly without implying the recipient attended.
- State what is now available: recording, notes, slides, materials, or another supplied resource.
- Summarize two or three concrete things they can revisit.
- Put the primary recording or notes link where it is easy to find.
- Keep the message focused on follow-up; do not add unrelated newsletter sections.

Treat this as segmented operational follow-up, not automatically as a broadcast to the entire DataTalks.Club list.

## Style And Requirements

- Use short paragraphs and descriptive headings.
- Use bullets only for real learning outcomes, agenda items, or recap points.
- Prefer one primary CTA.
- Use linked text when the delivery format supports it; preserve the destination exactly.
- Avoid first-person singular, personal anecdotes, hype, artificial urgency, and unsupported sponsor claims.
- Do not promise jobs, certification, discounts, scarcity, or outcomes unless explicitly supplied.
- Do not create a subject line or preview text unless requested.

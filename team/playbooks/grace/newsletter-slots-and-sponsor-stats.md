# Playbook: Newsletter slot sales & sponsor stats (Grace, Valeriia)

Source: `team/telegram-analysis/reports/dtc-chat-grace-analysis.md` (P6, P7,
taxonomy 3.5, 3.11) and `team-email-analysis.md` (W2).

**Trigger**: sponsor inquiry (including Spam-folder rescues — Grace triages
the inbox), quarterly rebooking (e.g. Astronomer), or slot collisions.

## Products & pricing (2026 observed)

| Product | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Primary slot | €1,500 | Mondays only |
| Secondary slot | ~$1,000–1,200 | Mondays only |
| Standalone | €2,500 | any day except Monday; "Wednesday is the best slot" |
| Alexey's social post | €1,200 | Alexey is the author |
| Bundle | 5+ placements | −10% |

Volume discounts only; no single-blast discounts ("We don't do discounts on
blasts because we want to minimize the volume we send.").

## Slot cycle steps

1. Check the newsletter calendar sheet for free Monday slots.
2. Validate proposed dates against the Monday rule ("Just noticed these
   dates are mostly on Tuesdays. Since our slots are for Mondays…").
3. Propose 2–3 options in one email (avoid back-and-forth).
4. Draft reply for Alexey's approval.
5. T−14 days: send the copy-doc invite (T3) with submission deadline.
6. Chase copy: Grace bumps the deadline; Valeriia bumps with detail;
   graphics spec 1200×600. Valeriia reviews ("we ask our sponsors to avoid
   using 'we' or 'our' because it can confuse our readers").
7. Late copy → Valeriia's 2-hour ultimatum or send as-is; no-show on a
   reserved slot → her release warning (T19).
8. Standalones get a "[Test]" email first.
9. Monday ~10:00 CET send; "Your promotion is now live!" (T4).
10. T+1 week: stats (T5) — note summer dips proactively
    ("probably likely because of the summer break").
11. Invoice before/around the send; confirm paid (checklist step).

## Sponsor performance report (~1 month post-event)

1. Registrations by platform (Luma / Meetup / LinkedIn).
2. Live attendees (+ screenshot).
3. Per-post impressions/likes/reposts (before vs after event).
4. Newsletter views/clicks; YouTube views.
5. Structure "first before, then after" in a Google Sheet.
6. Draft email → approval → send.
7. Only DTC-attributable metrics ("We don't know which RSVPs are coming
   from where so we cannot really attribute it to us" — report views and
   clicks only).
8. Reused as sales collateral for future sponsors.

**Frequency**: ~weekly slot coordination; a stats report per sponsored event.

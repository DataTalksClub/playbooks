# Playbook: Event creation & multi-platform publishing (Grace)

Source: `team/telegram-analysis/reports/dtc-chat-grace-analysis.md` (P4,
taxonomy 3.7) and `team-email-analysis.md` (W3).

**Trigger**: sponsor contract closed, speaker confirmed, or
`/todo start a workshop process for {date} (I already create a time blocker
- check my calendar) {emails}`.

**Canonical slots**: workshops Tue/Thu 16:30–18:00 CET; webinars/podcasts
Mon/Tue 12:30–13:30 CET; Lightning Lessons 17:00–19:00 CET.

## Steps

1. Send the workshop-requirements Google doc to the speaker (T2) + calendar
   invite (fixed Zoom room + doc link, "(Proposed)" until confirmed).
2. Create the Luma event:
   - cover from the Canva template with sponsor logos
   - registration form includes "Company Name" (added 2026-03-27) and the
     email-opt-in checkbox for sponsors
   - a placeholder event is OK when content isn't decided yet
3. Mirror the event to Meetup (Berlin + global groups), LinkedIn, X.
4. Add to the website via Airtable (see website playbook).
5. **Verify time/timezone across every platform + calendar** — mandatory
   double-check step added 2026-05-30 after a midnight-timezone Meetup error.
   Known incident classes: Luma vs calendar mismatch, wrong month, stale
   Meetup copy after a Luma deletion, hidden events not announced.
6. Chase speakers who haven't filled the doc (T18 + deadline framing:
   fill by Friday → Monday newsletter).
7. Announce ≥1 month before the date (2026-03-26 rule); Valeriia handles
   the copy and social announcements.
8. Reminders: ~1 week + day before (T6 with share ask).
9. Live day: monitor; fix issues (Zoom links were rotated 2026-06-08 after
   a leak; LLs got a dedicated link 2026-06-30).
10. Post-event: run the video pipeline; stats to the sponsor (T7b or full
    report); invoice via the invoicing playbook; detailed stats report
    ~1 month later (process added 2026-02-18).

**Access note**: Grace's own Luma account was deactivated 2026-08-31 (cost);
she uses public links or Alexey's account — "there's a process document that
describes how you can connect to my account".

**Alexey decisions**: date/time, final title/description (often iterated),
cover choice (delegated to Valeriia).

**Frequency**: ~3–5 events/month.

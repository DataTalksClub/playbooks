# Playbook: Podcast guest pipeline (Grace)

Source: `team/telegram-analysis/reports/dtc-chat-grace-analysis.md` (P1) and
`team/telegram-analysis/reports/team-email-analysis.md` (W1).
Evidence dates cited in those reports. Current as of Oct 2026.

**Trigger**: Alexey sends `/todo podcast <email>` (± LinkedIn URL, notes), a
guest is recommended, or a guest pitches inbound.

**Canonical slot**: Mon/Tue 12:30–13:30 CET.

## Steps

1. Check Alexey's calendar for 1–2 free canonical slots.
2. Ask Alexey: "Will {date} at 12:30–1:30 PM CET works for you?" — attach a
   calendar screenshot (he explicitly likes this).
3. Send the outreach email (template T1: intro + topics ask + published-work
   ask + slot proposal).
4. On guest confirmation:
   - send the calendar invite from Grace's account, "(Proposed)" removed,
     body = fixed Zoom room + "Podcast document: {doc-link}"
     (mandatory since 2026-03-17: every podcast invite carries the
     questions-doc link)
   - start the questions doc; ask the guest for bio + questions they'd like
     Alexey to ask
5. If guest is slow with bio: generate it from their LinkedIn ("we just copy
   whatever they have in LinkedIn" — 2026-03-19 rule). Questions doc can be
   ChatGPT-generated from the LinkedIn profile (2026-08-21).
6. Nudge with T18 if the doc stays unfilled.
7. ~1 week before: reminder + share-the-registration-link ask (T6).
8. Day before: final reminder.
9. After recording:
   - Alexey uploads video/audio or forwards the Zoom link → follow the
     video pipeline playbook (upload, cut, transcript, timecodes,
     description)
   - audio → Dropbox → Dave (Podcast Engineers) for editing
   - schedule Spotify release for a Friday (episodes numbered s23eNN)
   - add the episode to Airtable so it appears on the website
10. Next day: thank-you email with YouTube link (T7a); ask for
    description links.
11. Report links to Alexey + the content channel (with Valeriia).

**Frequency**: ~2–4 guests/month (15 `/todo podcast` in 9 months + inbound).

**Alexey decisions**: slot choice; removing sensitive parts from recordings
(e.g., cut a Meta affiliation mention on request).

**Endings observed**: links received + thanks; ghost → 1–2 bumps → cancel
with "Cancelled event, thank you."; warm postpone ("We'll reach out to you
in November and good luck with your studies!").

**Evidence to attach**: YouTube/Spotify/doc links; episode schedule lists
(e.g., the Season 23 list with dates and titles).

# Playbook: Sponsor copy review & outreach (Valeriia)

Source: `team/telegram-analysis/reports/valeriia-analysis.md` §3H;
`team-email-analysis.md` (T15, T16, T19). Evidence dates cited there.

## A. Sponsor copy review (the weekly grind)

**Trigger**: a sponsor's template/copy arrives (via Grace's T3 doc or
email) for a newsletter slot, sponsored post, or workshop promo.

**Rule check**: no "we/us/our" (house rule — readers must not confuse the
voice); length ≤~6 lines; no jargon pileups; a clear CTA; working links;
graphics 1200×600 where applicable.

**Steps**:
1. Check the copy against the rules.
2. If weak → propose a concrete rewrite (Nebius pattern: "ваш вариант не
   очень, вот наш, если вам ок, то мы его используем", 2026-02-09) and/or
   banner variants (3 variants for Meritmap, 2026-08-20; Pathway rewrite
   2026-08-17).
3. Give edit rationale in persuasive terms (Collate rewrite: "catch their
   attention with the efficiency improvement (from 22 minutes to 90
   seconds)", 2026-08-21).
4. Test email ("[Test] …") for standalones.
5. Approval (Grace/Alexey loop) → publish → collect per-post clicks into
   the sponsor report (she extended Grace's stats template with per-post
   clicks, 2026-04-24).

**Hard deadline authority** (she owns this): "Please respond within 2
hours. Otherwise, we will send it as is because we cannot delay the send
due to the other sponsor." (2026-08-17).

## B. Silent-sponsor escalation ladder (codified 2026-04-24/27)

For a sponsor occupying a slot who has gone quiet:
1. Reminder.
2. CC the boss as a last resort.
3. Replace the slot — possibly with a free goodwill promo — and inform the
   sponsor (T19 release warning: "we treat it as confirmed… keep it or
   release it").
4. Empty slot default → course promo.

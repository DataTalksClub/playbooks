## What We'll Cover

Alexey will walk through:

- What you'll learn: ML fundamentals, regression, classification, evaluation, trees, ensembles, deep learning, deployment, and model serving
- How the course works: videos, homework, deadlines, leaderboard, peer review, and certificate path
- What you'll build: midterm and capstone projects with deployed models that can become portfolio pieces
- How to stay on track: pacing, learning in public, and getting unstuck when the material gets dense
- Where to get help: DataTalks.Club Slack, Telegram updates, FAQ, and community support

You'll also hear practical advice on how to approach the course, keep momentum, and turn the projects into something you can show later.

## Is This Course for You?

ML Zoomcamp is a free 4-month course for people who want to become practical machine learning engineers. It starts with the basics and gradually moves toward production-style work: model evaluation, deployment, deep learning, serverless inference, Kubernetes, and model serving.

You should be comfortable with programming, the command line, Git, and Docker basics. You do not need previous machine learning experience, advanced math, cloud experience, or a degree.

## About Event

**Join Alexey Grigorev and Alexander Guschin for a live preview of the "Bot or Not" MLOps Zoomcamp Competition.**

This session will walk you through everything you need to know to participate in the competition and apply your MLOps skills in practice.

What to expect:

*   **Competition overview:** How the challenge works and what you’ll build

*   **Task breakdown:** What’s expected from your model and how to get started

*   **Platform walkthrough**: How to deploy, monitor, and evaluate your ML system in a live environment

This practical challenge is designed to help you go beyond theory:

*   Deploy your own ML model

*   Handle real-time requests from a live service

*   Monitor key operational metrics like uptime and latency

*   Track model performance with live feedback on accuracy and more

Tune in live to get all the details and ask your questions directly to Alexey and Alexander.

## About Event

Join us for an interactive Q&A session about the MLOps Zoomcamp, our free online course designed to teach the practical aspects of productionizing machine learning (ML) services. This includes everything from training and experimenting to model deployment and monitoring.

Hosted by Alexey Grigorev, founder of DataTalks.Club, this Q&A event is designed specifically for those who have questions about the course and want to learn more before signing up.

This event is perfect for anyone curious about the MLOps Zoomcamp and considering signing up. You'll have the opportunity to:

*   Ask direct questions and gain insights into the practical challenges of ML operations

*   Understand the course curriculum, including topics and technologies covered

*   Learn about course assignments, scoring, and how to receive feedback

*   Discover projects you can add to your portfolio

*   Get tips on how to navigate the course materials effectively

Don't miss this chance to get your ML engineering questions answered directly from the instructor who designed the course!

## About Event

From Picture to Podcast - Alexey Grigorev

What if everyday objects weren’t so ordinary? In this talk, Alexey will share how he and his son built a GenAI-powered system that transforms photos of common objects into horror stories—automatically generating a new podcast episode every day. He'll dive into the process, deployment, automation, and key lessons learned.

## About Event

**Build with LLM Applications for the Community - Alexey Grigorev**

We’re starting the DataTalks.Club Summer 2025 AI Hackathon, a 9-week opportunity to turn your LLM knowledge into useful LLM-based tools for the community.

In this launch webinar, we will cover:

*   The goal and structure of the hackathon

*   How to submit and select project ideas: [Submit your idea here](https://airtable.com/appjxMzaGej3KHwEh/shr12Sx3SGsStvjEd?utm_source=luma)

*   Criteria for project selection

*   Examples of potential project directions

*   Team creation process

*   Q&A session to clarify logistics and expectations

If you’re still unsure what to build, this session is a great place to get inspiration, feedback, and guidance.

About the Speaker

**Alexey Grigorev** is the creator of DataTalks.Club and instructor of the popular Zoomcamp series. He designs and teaches practical AI/ML workflows used by thousands of learners globally. In this workshop, Alexey brings his hands-on teaching style to the emerging field of agentic LLMs, helping you build useful assistants from scratch.

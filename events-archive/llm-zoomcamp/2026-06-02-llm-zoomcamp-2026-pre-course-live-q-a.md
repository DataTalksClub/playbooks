## About Event

Join us for a live Q&A with **Alexey Grigorev**, founder of DataTalks.Club and creator of LLM Zoomcamp.

[LLM Zoomcamp](https://github.com/DataTalksClub/llm-zoomcamp/tree/main?utm_source=luma) is a free, hands-on course on building practical LLM applications with RAG, agents, vector search, evaluation, monitoring, and more. The 2026 live cohort starts on **June 8, 2026**, and this session is a chance to ask questions before you join.

We’ll cover:

*   What you’ll build during the course

*   Who the course is for and what prerequisites you need

*   How the live cohort works: deadlines, homework, leaderboard, peer review, and certificates

*   What to expect from the capstone project

*   How to get the most out of the DataTalks.Club community

*   Your questions about LLMs, RAG, agents, and learning path

The course is free and open-source. Basic Python and command-line experience are enough to get started.

Bring your questions and join us live.

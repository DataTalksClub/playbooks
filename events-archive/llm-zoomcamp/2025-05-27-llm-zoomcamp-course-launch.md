# LLM Zoomcamp Course Launch

- **Type:** Webinar
- **Date:** Tuesday, May 27, 2025
- **Time:** 17:00 Europe/Berlin
- **Instructor:** Alexey Grigorev
- **Luma:** https://lu.ma/ei5wx9ck
- **Course:** LLM Zoomcamp

## About Event

Join us for the live launch of **[LLM Zoomcamp](https://airtable.com/appPPxkgYLH06Mvbw/shr7WtxHEPXxaui0Q?utm_source=luma)**, a free online course on real-life applications of large language models (LLMs).

Alexey Grigorev, the course creator, will officially launch the program live. He'll walk you through the course structure, key concepts, and what you’ll build, including an AI bot that answers questions based on your own knowledge base.

If you're already enrolled, this is the perfect way to get oriented and meet the team. If you're still exploring, get a feel for what it’s like to be part of LLM Zoomcamp.

You'll learn:

*   What’s included in the course: topics, projects, tools

*   How assignments, feedback, and scoring work

*   How to approach the material effectively, even without prior AI/ML experience

*   What it means to learn together in a global, supportive community

And you'll get the chance to ask Alexey your questions live.

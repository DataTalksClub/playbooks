## About Event

We’re running an open Q&A session to introduce the **[Open-Source LLM Zoomcamp](https://github.com/DataTalksClub/open-source-llm-zoomcamp?utm_source=luma)**, a free hands-on course on building, fine-tuning, and deploying open-source large language models (LLMs) using cutting-edge hardware like AMD’s MI300x GPUs.

This event is perfect for anyone curious about the Open-Source LLM Zoomcamp and considering registering.

You'll have the opportunity to:

*   Ask direct questions and gain insights into large language models

*   Learn about course assignments, scoring, and how to receive feedback

*   Get tips on how to navigate the course materials effectively

Ask your questions directly to the instructor, Alexey Grigorev, who designed the course!

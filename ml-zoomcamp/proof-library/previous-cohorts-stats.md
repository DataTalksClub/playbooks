## Claims These Numbers Support

Safe to say:

- Over 2,000 people took part in the 2025 cohort.
- More than 400 people graduated in 2025.
- Students submitted more than 880 projects in 2025, and 766 of them passed.
- Around 87% of submitted projects passed.

Do not say:

- Anything about completion *rate*. Leaderboard entries are not the same as registrations, and we do not have a registration figure for 2025 in this file. Comparing 403 graduates against 2,381 leaderboard entries would be misleading.
- That project pass rates are course-wide success rates. They measure submitted projects, not everyone who started.
- Anything about how many people *finished all ten homeworks*. The per-module figures are submission counts, not per-person completion.

## How to Refresh

Ask the course management agent project for the previous cohort's graduates, leaderboard, and homework and project submissions. The submission records contain student emails, so keep counts only. Never copy the records into this repository.

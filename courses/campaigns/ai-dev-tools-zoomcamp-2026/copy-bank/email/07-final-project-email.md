# Final Project: Bring the Whole Workflow Together

Hello there,

You have reached the part of the course where the modules become one complete workflow.

For the final project, choose a problem that interests you and build a working application with AI assistance. The agent can help you plan and implement, but you remain responsible for the product decisions, architecture, review, tests, security boundaries, and final result.

Read the [project requirements](https://datatalks.club/docs/courses/ai-dev-tools-zoomcamp/project/) before you settle on the scope. The project should include a frontend and backend, an OpenAPI contract, persistent storage, tests, containers, deployment, and reproducible instructions. You should also explain how you used prompts, context, skills, or agents.

## Choose a Submission Window

You need one passing project for the certificate.

For the first window, submit through [Project 1](https://courses.datatalks.club/ai-dev-tools-2026/project/project1) by **October 20, 2026 at 01:00 Europe/Madrid** (**October 19 at 23:00 UTC**).

For the second window, submit through [Project 2](https://courses.datatalks.club/ai-dev-tools-2026/project/project2) by **November 10, 2026 at 00:00 Europe/Madrid** (**November 9 at 23:00 UTC**).

Choose the window that gives you enough time to build something complete and reviewable. A smaller deployed project with clear documentation is better than a large unfinished one.

## What Happens After Submission

After you submit, the next step is peer review. Complete all three assigned reviews on time; missing the required reviews means the project does not qualify for the certificate.

Read the [peer-review guide](https://datatalks.club/docs/courses/zoomcamp-logistics/peer-review/) before the review period begins. Reviewing other projects is also a useful way to compare architectures, documentation, and AI workflows.

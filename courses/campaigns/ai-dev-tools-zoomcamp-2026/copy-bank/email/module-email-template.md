# Module Email Assembly Guide

The 2026 module emails in this folder are fully resolved and ready for editorial review. They do not use template placeholders.

For future cohorts, create concrete module emails from the target course's `course.yaml` and include:

- A short connection to what learners did in the previous module.
- A human explanation of why the current module matters.
- Materials, recordings, and supporting links placed in the paragraphs where they are relevant.
- A source-backed explanation of the homework, with its instructions, submission URL, and deadline.
- A tailored social post template learners can use to share their progress.
- A preview of the next module and how it builds on the current work.
- Help links in a closing sentence rather than a large generic link list.

Remove the browser-view line unless the sending platform has already generated a real public URL. Do not publish unresolved template syntax, dynamic countdowns, or open/closed status snapshots.

For the 2026 cohort, the current module resource map is `urls.cohort_2026_module_resources`, homework locations are under `delivery.homework.source`, and deadlines and submission pages are under `cohort_schedule`.

# Your Path to the AI Dev Tools Zoomcamp Certificate

Hello there,

You have worked through the course modules and reached the final project stage. Here is the short version of what counts for the certificate.

Homework is for practice and is not required. To earn the certificate during the 2026 live cohort, you need to submit one passing final project and complete all three assigned peer reviews on time.

Your project should show a complete, reproducible application and make your engineering judgment visible: what you asked AI to do, what context you provided, how you reviewed the result, which tests you ran, and which decisions or permissions remained human-controlled.

You can check your submission status on the [course platform](https://courses.datatalks.club/ai-dev-tools-2026/). The [project requirements](https://datatalks.club/docs/courses/ai-dev-tools-zoomcamp/project/), [peer-review guide](https://datatalks.club/docs/courses/zoomcamp-logistics/peer-review/), and [certificate rules](https://datatalks.club/docs/courses/zoomcamp-logistics/certification/) explain the details.

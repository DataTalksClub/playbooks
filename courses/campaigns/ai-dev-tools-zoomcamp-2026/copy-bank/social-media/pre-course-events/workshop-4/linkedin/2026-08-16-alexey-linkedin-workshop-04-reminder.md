This Tuesday, Grace Lordel Domisiw and I are running Workshop 4 of AI Dev Tools Zoomcamp: DevOps and Observability for AI-Built Apps.

I will start from a deployed app and build the production feedback loop around it: OpenTelemetry, metrics, logs, traces, Grafana dashboards, alerts, runbooks, evidence packets, read-only agent investigation, bounded actions, escalation, recovery verification, and audit.

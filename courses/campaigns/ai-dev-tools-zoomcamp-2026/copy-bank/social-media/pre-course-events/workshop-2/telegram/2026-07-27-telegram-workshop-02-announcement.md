Next Monday, we are running Workshop 2 of AI Dev Tools Zoomcamp 2026:

Build and Ship an AI-Assisted Full-Stack App

This workshop is for anyone who can get AI to generate code but needs a clearer way to turn that code into a complete application.

Alexey will take one project from the initial idea to deployment. He will show what to define before implementation, what to ask AI to produce, how to use that output in the next stage, and what to check before moving on.

Join to see a development process you can reuse for your own projects. The goal is to keep AI-assisted work understandable, testable, and ready to ship as the application grows.

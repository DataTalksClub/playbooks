## This Monday: building a complete application with AI

This Monday, I am running Workshop 2 for AI Dev Tools Zoomcamp:

Build and Ship an AI-Assisted Full-Stack App

I will take one application from the initial idea to deployment and show how I organize the work around AI coding tools.

At each stage, I will explain what I need to decide first, what I ask AI to produce, and what I review before I accept the result and move forward.

Join if you already use AI for individual coding tasks but want to see how those tasks fit into a complete development process. The goal is to keep the project understandable and testable while moving faster through implementation.

You will see a sequence you can reuse for your own applications and for the course final project.

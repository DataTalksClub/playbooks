Join us on August 3 for a free live workshop:

Build and Ship an AI-Assisted Full-Stack App

This workshop is for developers who use AI coding tools for individual tasks and want to understand how to use them across a complete project.

Alexey Grigorev will take one application from the initial idea to deployment. He will explain what to define before asking AI to write code, how to carry decisions from one stage to the next, and what to review, test, and debug before shipping.

You will see a practical development sequence you can reuse for your own applications and for the AI Dev Tools Zoomcamp final project.

# Post

Join us on August 17 for a free live workshop: Coding Agent Capabilities: MCP, Skills, Plugins, and Custom Agents.

Modern coding agents offer many ways to extend what they know and what they can do. The difficult part is deciding whether a workflow needs project instructions, an MCP connection, a reusable skill, a subagent, a hook, a plugin, or a custom agent.

Alexey Grigorev will organize these mechanisms around the problems they solve and build an extension pack around a real application. You will see how context, tools, reusable procedures, specialist roles, and automated checks fit together.

Join if you want to customize a coding agent for your own repository without losing control of permissions, review, or security. Monday, August 17 at 2:00 PM GMT+2. RSVP: https://luma.com/ap4l3qlj

Passing tests and successful deployment don't mean your app works in production.

It just means the code got deployed.

To know if it actually works, you need three things connected:

- A metric that shows the error rate spiked
- A trace that shows which service failed
- A log that explains why it failed

Most observability setups give you separate dashboards. You see the spike, then hunt through logs, then try to connect it to a specific request.

In Workshop 4 of AI Dev Tools Zoomcamp, I will show how to instrument a single endpoint so that the metric, trace, and log all capture the same failed request.

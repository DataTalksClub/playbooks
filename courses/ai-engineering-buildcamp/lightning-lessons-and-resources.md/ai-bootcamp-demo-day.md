## What you'll learn

### See Agentic Systems in Action

Go beyond basic chatbots. Witness how students have combined RAG with Agentic workflows.

### Discover Real-World AI Implementations

From coding assistants to deep research agents, explore a diverse portfolio of capstone projects.

### Understand the "Engineering" Behind AI

These aren't just toy prototypes. Learn how students tested, evaluated and monitored their agents.

## Why this topic matters

We shift from passive chatbots to active agents. These projects demonstrate that building reliable AI requires engineering rigor: testing, evaluations, and monitoring. Watch how students build agentic flows to solve real-world problems. Whether you're hiring or looking for inspiration, come see the practical future of AI engineering.

## What you'll learn

### Job Market Analysis of ~5,000 AI Engineering Jobs

Uncover real employer demand data to separate high-value tools like RAG and agents from declining market noise.

### Core Responsibilities Across Technical Roles

Map daily workflows across roles to pinpoint where traditional model training ends and software integration begins.

### Real Company Examples of AI Team Operations

Audit actual production setups to see how top tech companies structure, deploy, and scale their AI engineering units.

## Why this topic matters

The AI job market evolves too fast for outdated advice. By analyzing 5,000 real job postings, this lesson cuts through market noise to show you the exact technical skills employers demand right now. Professionally, you will stop guessing what recruiters want, gain clear direction on how engineering roles differ, and position your skill set for the highest-value AI engineering opportunities.

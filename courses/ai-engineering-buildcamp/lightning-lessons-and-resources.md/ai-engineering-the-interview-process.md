## What you'll learn

### Analyze Real-World Interview Data

Identify current hiring trends and frequently asked questions based on crowdsourced candidate experiences and research.

### Master Technical and Conceptual Questions

Learn how to articulate complex AI concepts and defend technical decisions regarding model selection and architecture.

### Solve Live Coding Challenges

Apply your skills in real-time by writing clean, efficient code for common AI-specific technical interview tasks.

## Why this topic matters

The AI interview landscape is now a "wild west" blending traditional software engineering with niche machine learning concepts. By studying real stories from platforms like Reddit and X, you eliminate the fear of the unknown. This prepares you for the professional reality of technical screenings, helping you grow from a candidate who knows AI into a professional who can prove it under pressure.

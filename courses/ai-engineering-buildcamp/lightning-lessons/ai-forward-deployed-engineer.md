Forward-deployed AI jobs are growing at twice the rate of the general market. Companies are desperate to close the gap between working prototypes and real production systems. Mastering this role combines client-facing problem solving with hands-on software engineering. You will gain a massive career edge by becoming the crucial engineer who actually makes enterprise AI work in the real world.

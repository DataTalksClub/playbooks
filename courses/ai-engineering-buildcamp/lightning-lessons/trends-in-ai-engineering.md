## Why this topic matters

AI engineering shifts every few months, making static frameworks obsolete overnight. Understanding current trends keeps you from building on legacy tools or over-engineering solutions. By mastering where the stack is heading, you can make smarter architectural choices today, drastically cut compute overhead, and future-proof your career as a forward-thinking engineer who delivers production-ready AI.

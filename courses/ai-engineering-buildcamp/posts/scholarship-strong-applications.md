I launched a new scholarship application for my upcoming AI Engineering Buildcamp.

Want to get it? Here are 5 things that separate strong applications from the rest:

1. Specific problem statement

Strong applications describe a concrete problem, explain who benefits from solving it, and show why training is needed now.

2. Existing work

The scholarship is for people who want to scale existing impact. Applications that usually stood out have ongoing projects or concrete systems they were building.

3. Clear alignment

How does the buildcamp connect to your current work? Why production-focused training matters for your specific situation? One-line answers usually get filtered out.

4. Likelihood of completion

The program is demanding, so I look for signals that someone can commit:

- Prior long-term projects
- Concrete plans
- Time allocation details
- Existing accountability structures

5. Depth over length

Long applications didn't win automatically. Authentic, focused responses with real substance beat generic essays.

If you're applying for a scholarship to my AI Engineering Buildcamp, consider applying for a partial scholarship instead of a full one.

Here's why:

Competition for full scholarships is extremely high. We received 2,500+ applications for the previous cohort.

If you can cover even 30-50% of the course fee yourself, your chances of receiving a scholarship increase significantly.

Here's a comparison:

Option A: Award 5 full scholarships at $1,799 each. That's 5 people trained.

Option B: Award 15 partial scholarships covering 50-70% each. That's 15 people trained.

When applicants co-invest, we distribute limited funds to more students.

Historically, applicants who financially commit also have higher completion rates.

If you're on the fence about whether you can afford it:

- Apply for a partial scholarship
- Explain what you can realistically contribute
- Show how the investment fits your situation
- Focus on the impact you'll create after completing the course

The course fee is $1,799. Partial scholarships range from 30% to 70% off.

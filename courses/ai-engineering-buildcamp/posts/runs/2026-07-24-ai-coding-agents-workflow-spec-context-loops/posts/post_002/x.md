1/5

I often start an AI coding project before opening the coding agent.

I use a chat assistant for scoping:

"Ask me one question at a time and keep your output short."

2/5

This keeps the conversation manageable.

Instead of a wall of text, I answer one product decision at a time.

3/5

In the workshop, we turned:

"A tool for weekly feedback for projects"

into decisions about users, anonymity, feedback visibility, voting, retrospectives, and action items.

4/5

This is product work.

The goal is to remove ambiguity before the coding agent starts making assumptions.

5/5

Use AI to clarify what you want before using AI to build it.

Agent demos often stop at the agent loop:

Plan.
Call tools.
Return an answer.

Production needs the layer around it.

Workshop with Nicholas Lotz: [registration link]

2/5

That layer handles the boring failures:

The process dies.
A deploy interrupts the run.
A tool call times out.
Approval arrives later.
You need to see what happened.

3/5

This is what separates a useful demo from a service people can depend on.

The agent has to keep state, recover from failures, retry safely, and show its execution history.

- Resuming after failures

- Human approval steps

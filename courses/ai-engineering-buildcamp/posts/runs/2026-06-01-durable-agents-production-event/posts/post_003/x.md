1/5

An agent prepares a refund.

A human needs to approve it first.

The approval arrives 3 hours later, after a deploy.

What should the workflow remember?

Workshop: [registration link]

2/5

Human approval looks simple in a diagram:

Propose action.
Review.
Continue.

Production makes it harder because the workflow may wait for hours or days.

3/5

The agent needs a way to pause, keep the proposed action, and continue when the approval event arrives.

Otherwise the approval step becomes a fragile point in the workflow.

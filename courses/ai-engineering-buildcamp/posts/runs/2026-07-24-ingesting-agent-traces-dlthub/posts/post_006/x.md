A local dashboard is a good validation step.

A team dashboard needs deployment.

2/5

In the workshop, we started locally:

Read agent trace files. Load them into DuckDB. Open a Marimo report. Check whether the schema and charts make sense.

3/5

That human validation matters.

Do the charts match your usage? Are the important fields queryable? Did nested JSON become a usable schema?

4/5

For a team, the next step is scheduled ingestion, a cloud/private destination, and a deployed report that does not depend on someone's laptop.

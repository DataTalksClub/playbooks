## Context

This workshop is about solving a real AI engineer take-home assignment by building an investment coaching bot. The material focuses on practical engineering decisions around a PydanticAI CLI agent, adapting when market data APIs are not available, using free SEC EDGAR data, cleaning up bad agent-generated tests, adding manual evaluations with an LLM judge, and wrapping the result as a Telegram bot.

1/6

When I get a vague AI engineer take-home assignment, I do not start by coding.

I start by making the assignment less vague.

2/6

The workshop task was:

"Build a Telegram bot for investment coaching with safety filtering."

That is not enough detail to implement well.

3/6

Before coding, clarify:

- Who is the user?
- What should the bot answer?
- What should it refuse?
- Which data sources are allowed?
- What is the smallest useful version?

4/6

For this project, the key question was not the framework.

It was whether we can build something useful with public company data while being clear that this is not financial advice.

5/6

The scoped product became:

An educational investment coach that explains public company data and refuses personalized buy/sell/hold advice.

6/6

A good take-home does not only show that you can write code.

It shows that you can turn an unclear request into a scoped engineering problem.

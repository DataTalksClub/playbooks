We start Module 6 on Decision Trees and Ensemble Learning.

Linear models are a good start, but tree-based models often work better on tabular data. In this module, we build a credit risk scoring model and combine many trees into stronger models.

We cover:

- Decision trees and how they are learned
- Random forests
- Gradient boosting with XGBoost
- Hyperparameter tuning
- Feature importance
- Selecting the best model

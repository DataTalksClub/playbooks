We start the capstone projects.

You have now covered the full path from framing a problem to serving a model on Kubernetes. The capstone is where you apply it to a larger end-to-end project of your choice.

There are two capstone windows. For a certificate, you need two passing projects: the midterm and one capstone, or both capstones.

The requirements are the same as for the midterm:

- A new public dataset, not one from the lectures or homework
- Data preparation, EDA, and feature importance analysis
- Several trained and tuned models, and the one you selected
- A training script and a prediction service
- Dependency and environment files, and a Dockerfile
- A deployed model, or a video or screenshot of the running service

# Alexey Audience

Primary audience:

- Data scientists
- ML engineers
- Data engineers
- MLOps professionals
- AI engineers
- Software engineers
- Tech professionals aiming to advance in data science, ML engineering, and AI

Audience expectations:

- Practical, evidence-based content
- Data-driven analysis
- Skill breakdowns
- Tool recommendations
- Event and course announcements
- Clear explanations of production trade-offs
- Concrete examples from real workflows

Content that tends to work:

- Lessons from real incidents or mistakes
- Practical learning paths
- Tool comparisons with trade-offs
- Breakdowns of job market patterns or interview expectations
- Course/workshop announcements with specific outcomes
- Community stories and learner outcomes
- Engineering concepts explained simply

Avoid content that feels:

- Generic
- Motivational without substance
- Overly polished or corporate
- Hype-driven
- Detached from hands-on engineering work

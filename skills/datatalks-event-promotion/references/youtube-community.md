# YouTube Community

Use this reference for event announcements, reminders, educational posts, recaps, and repurposed text or video posts published in the DataTalks.Club YouTube Community feed.

Treat YouTube Community as a video-audience channel, not as a copy of LinkedIn or X. Preserve the campaign angle and facts, then adapt the opening, context, formatting, and CTA for people who already follow DataTalks.Club on YouTube.

## Core Style

Prefer:

- A direct opening that says what is live, upcoming, available to watch, or useful to learn.
- Short paragraphs and compact bullets.
- The full event or video title on its own line when that improves scanning.
- Clear references to the live session, recording, demonstration, walkthrough, or clip when relevant.
- Date, time, timezone, and link on easy-to-find lines.

Avoid:

- LinkedIn-specific framing, X thread numbering, or references to another platform.
- A long personal setup from an Alexey post; DataTalks.Club owns the YouTube channel voice.
- Saying `watch below`, `link in bio`, or `see the thread` unless the actual post format supports it.
- Hashtag blocks, keyword stuffing, and clickbait.

## Adaptation By Post Type

Announcement:

- Name the live workshop, event, or premiere early.
- Explain who should watch and what they will see demonstrated or discussed.
- End with the live date/time and registration or stream link.

Educational post:

- Teach one source-backed idea first.
- Connect it to the upcoming session or existing recording near the end.
- Keep the explanation self-contained; the link should extend the idea, not rescue unclear copy.

Reminder:

- Lead with the timing and title.
- Give one specific reason to watch.
- Keep it shorter than the announcement.

Recap or repurposed post:

- State what viewers can now watch or revisit.
- Describe the specific topic, moment, or takeaway in the recording or clip.
- Link to the recording, notes, or materials when supplied.

## Delivery Check

- The post makes sense to a YouTube subscriber without seeing the LinkedIn/X source.
- Video-oriented language is used only when a live stream, recording, or clip actually exists.
- Event and link details match the source.
- The post is a channel adaptation, not a new unsupported campaign angle.

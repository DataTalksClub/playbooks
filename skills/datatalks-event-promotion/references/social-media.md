# Social Media Routing

Use this reference to choose the correct owner and platform guidance for LinkedIn and X deliverables. Account ownership determines the voice; the platform determines the format and technical constraints.

## Choose The Owner First

DataTalks.Club account:

- Read `datatalks-social-media.md` for the community voice and account-level requirements.
- Read `linkedin.md` for a LinkedIn deliverable.
- Read `x.md` for an X deliverable.

Alexey's account:

- Use `$social-content-studio` for drafting, reviewing, revising, and saving the post. That skill and its Alexey references are the source of truth for his voice, audience, personal framing, artifact format, and media workflow.
- Read `linkedin.md` or `x.md` here only for the event campaign handoff and the account's platform constraints.
- Do not apply `datatalks-style.md` or `datatalks-social-media.md` to Alexey's post.

If one campaign needs both owners, create separate deliverables. Keep the event facts, campaign angle, dates, names, claims, and CTA links aligned, but adapt the narration and structure for each owner.

## Ownership Boundary

Alexey can write from personal experience and relationships when the source supports them: why he invited a speaker, what he will do in a workshop, a problem he faced, or how he knows the guest. Never invent this context.

DataTalks.Club has no single personal narrator. Its posts can use storytelling and audience pain points, but the story should be about practitioners, community learning, the event topic, or the speaker's source-backed expertise.

Do not turn an Alexey post into a DataTalks.Club post by only replacing `I` with `we`. Reframe the post around what the community can learn or do.

## Cross-Platform Handoff

For Alexey, LinkedIn and X may use the same long-form core post because his X account has Premium. Platform-specific editing is optional unless the user requests it or the presentation clearly benefits from it.

For DataTalks.Club, create a long-form LinkedIn version and a standard-limit X thread. Every DTC X deliverable is a thread, and every post in it must meet the standard account limit. Do not paste the LinkedIn copy into one over-limit X post.

# LinkedIn

Use this reference for LinkedIn deliverables after selecting the account owner in `social-media.md`.

## Format

Treat LinkedIn as the long-form social version for this workflow. Use enough space to explain the practical context, the event's relevance, and the next step, but do not add length only because the platform allows it.

Prefer:

- A direct opening before event logistics.
- Short paragraphs that each add new information.
- Bullets for learning outcomes, agenda items, tools, or takeaways.
- Visible event details and a clear CTA near the end.
- One campaign angle per post.

Avoid:

- A wall of text.
- Copying a short X thread into LinkedIn with thread numbering or repeated context.
- Platform references such as `thread below` or `in the next post`.
- Hashtags unless requested or required by an existing campaign.

## Event Post Check

- The opening gives the reader a reason to continue.
- The post explains why the topic matters before or alongside the promotion.
- Learning outcomes and speaker claims come from the source.
- Date, time, timezone, format, and link are preserved when supplied.
- A reminder is meaningfully shorter than the main announcement.

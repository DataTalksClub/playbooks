## Data Engineers Guide to AI Context Engineering with Airflow

AI doesn’t average out small errors. It compounds them. Every stale or contradictory piece of context adds risk to each step downstream, and most teams have no system to catch it.

Download this 89-page data engineering guide to AI context for best practices & tips to keep agents accurate and reliable.

- Build pipelines using Airflow's @task.agent, HITLOperator, and asset-aware scheduling to extract, embed, and load context to storage for AI agents
- Detect staleness, resolve contradictions, prune low-value chunks
- Capture decision traces and distill them into context graphs

## Live workshop: Building AI Agent Workflows

AI agents work great when they can do focused work, but they become harder to use when people and several agents need to work on the same task. The context gets split across chat, terminal sessions, dashboards, and other places.

On September 15, Louis Amaudruz will run a hands-on workshop on building multi-agent workflow. He will show how to:

- Define a real workflow where people and agents collaborate
- Bring existing agents into a shared room
- Build the workflow
- Keep context and history visible as people and agents join, leave, and hand off tasks
- Keep human review and approval in the loop
